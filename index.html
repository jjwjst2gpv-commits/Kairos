Rules

Kairos Rules

Mandatory for every Kairos drive

  1. Respect traffic laws at all times.
  2. Do not encourage speeding, racing or dangerous driving.
  3. Never drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any substance that affects driving ability.
  4. Keep a safe distance between vehicles.
  5. Follow the route and instructions communicated by the organizer.
  6. Do not share the exact meeting location with people who have not been approved.
  7. Respect every participant, their vehicle and their personal boundaries.
  8. Harassment, intimidation, discrimination and aggressive behavior are not tolerated.
  9. Do not pressure anyone to carry passengers or participate in activities they did not agree to.
  10. Every driver is responsible for their own vehicle, documents, insurance and legal compliance.
  11. Stop or leave the drive if conditions become unsafe.
  12. In an emergency, prioritize safety and contact the appropriate emergency services.

Kairos Rules are mandatory and cannot be edited or removed by the organizer.
